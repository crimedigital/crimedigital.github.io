Sin of Angels

A turn based strategy board game about outlaw motorcycle clubs fighting for control of Los Angeles. Browser based, no install, on desktop, tablet or phone.

Setup

  • Two to six clubs, any mix of human players and AI
  • One shared map of Los Angeles
  • Six rounds

What you do each round

ActionEffect
RecruitAdd Members and Recruits to your club
Move onto a SitePosition your Crew to work it
Work a SiteEarn Cash, Guns and Narcotics
TradeBuy and sell on The Silk Road
Manage FeloniesKeep the law off your back

Every one of those costs Orders, and Orders are the thing you never have enough of. A ceasefire with a rival club is always available, and it holds exactly as long as it pays to hold it.

Brawls

When two clubs land on the same Site it triggers a Brawl. You call for Backup, commit Guns, and roll dice, played out as a cinematic 3D fight rather than a card flip. Every committed Gun wounds one random Crew member of its target and sends them to the ER. The loser retreats to their Clubhouse.

How you win

Most Cash after round six. Territory is how you earn that Cash, it is not the win condition by itself. A tie goes to whoever holds the Envelope, which also sets turn order.

Four ways to play

  • Solo against the AI
  • Online multiplayer across devices
  • Local pass and play on one screen
  • Party mode, with the 3D board on a TV while everyone plays privately from their own phone

Status and pricing

Sin of Angels is in closed beta, and the beta is free to play. It is not publicly playable yet. Beta testers get in before release and their bug reports shape the finished game.

After the beta the game becomes paid. A single purchase is planned to cover browser, Steam and Android together, with cross-compatibility between them. No price has been set publicly yet.
