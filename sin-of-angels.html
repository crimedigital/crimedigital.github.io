CRIME Digital / Sin of Angels

What is the SOA Game?

The SOA Game is Sin of Angels: Hell City, a browser based outlaw motorcycle club strategy board game from CRIME Digital. It is not Sword Art Online. It is the SOA board game online: built for players hunting for games like the out-of-print Sons of Anarchy: Men of Mayhem board game, and it runs with no install on desktop, tablet or phone.

Sin of Angels: Hell City at a glance

  • Two to six clubs, any mix of human players and AI
  • One shared map of Los Angeles, six rounds
  • Browser based digital board game, no install
  • Currently in closed beta

Why people call it the SOA game

Same DNA as the Sons of Anarchy board game: rival clubs, area control over a gritty map, running guns and narcotics, cutting deals and breaking them the moment it pays to, and settling the rest in a fight. The Sons of Anarchy: Men of Mayhem box went out of print and copies go for collector money, so Sin of Angels: Hell City was built to fill that gap as an online board game you can actually get into.

How a round works

You spend Orders to recruit Members and Recruits, move onto Sites and work them for Cash, Guns and Narcotics, trade on The Silk Road, and manage your Felonies before the law catches up with you. Orders are the limiting resource and you never have enough of them. A ceasefire with a rival club is always available, and it holds exactly as long as it pays to hold it.

Brawls

When two clubs land on the same Site it triggers a Brawl. You call for Backup, commit Guns, and roll dice, played out as a cinematic 3D fight rather than a card flip. Every committed Gun wounds one random Crew member of its target and sends them to the ER. The loser retreats to their Clubhouse. The outcome resolves before the animation starts, so a slow device never changes who won.

How you win

Most Cash after round six. Territory is how you earn that Cash, it is not the win condition by itself. A tie goes to whoever holds the Envelope, which also sets turn order.

Four ways to play

  • Solo against the AI
  • Online multiplayer across devices
  • Local pass and play on one screen
  • Party mode, with the 3D board on a TV while everyone plays privately from their own phone

Status and pricing

Sin of Angels is in closed beta, and the beta is free to play. It is not publicly playable yet. Beta testers get in before release and their bug reports shape the finished game.

After the beta the game becomes paid. A single purchase is planned to cover browser, Steam and Android together, with cross-compatibility between them. No price has been set publicly yet.
