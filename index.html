Sin of Angels

Sin of Angels is a turn based strategy board game about outlaw motorcycle clubs fighting for control of Los Angeles. It runs entirely in a browser, with no install, on desktop, tablet or phone.

Two to six clubs, any mix of human players and AI, fight over one map across six rounds. Each round you spend Orders to recruit Members and Recruits, move onto Sites to work them for Cash, Guns and Narcotics, trade on The Silk Road, and manage your Felonies before the law catches up with you. You can hold a ceasefire with a rival club right up until the moment it pays to break it.

When two clubs land on the same Site it triggers a Brawl. You call for Backup, commit Guns, and roll dice, played out as a cinematic 3D fight rather than a card flip. Every committed Gun wounds one random Crew member of its target and sends them to the ER. The loser retreats to their Clubhouse.

The win condition is the most Cash after round six. Territory is how you earn that Cash, not the win condition by itself.

Ways to play

  • Solo against the AI
  • Online multiplayer across devices
  • Local pass and play on one screen
  • Party mode, with the 3D board on a TV while everyone plays privately from their own phone

Status

Sin of Angels is free and currently in closed beta. It is not publicly playable yet. Beta testers get in before release, and their bug reports shape the finished game.
