Sin of Angels

A turn based strategy board game about outlaw motorcycle clubs fighting for control of Los Angeles. It runs entirely in a browser, with no install, on desktop, tablet or phone.

Two to six clubs, any mix of human players and AI, fight for territory across six rounds. Each round you spend Orders to recruit Members and Recruits, move onto the map's Sites to work them for cash, guns and narcotics, trade on The Silk Road, and manage your Felonies before the law comes down on you.

When two clubs land on the same Site it triggers a Brawl: call for backup, pull guns, then roll, played out as a cinematic 3D fight rather than a card flip. Whoever holds the most Cash at the end of round six wins. Territory is how you earn that cash, not the win condition by itself.

Ways to play

  • Solo against the AI
  • Online multiplayer across devices
  • Local pass and play on one screen
  • Party mode: the 3D board on a TV while everyone plays privately from their own phone

Status

Sin of Angels is in closed beta, and the beta is free to play. It is not publicly playable yet. Beta testers get in early and their bug reports shape the finished game.

After the beta the game becomes paid. A single purchase is planned to cover browser, Steam and Android together, with cross-compatibility between them.
