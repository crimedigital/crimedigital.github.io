What is the SOA Game?

Sin of Angels: Hell City

Sin of Angels: Hell City, the SOA game, is an online board game you play in a browser with no install, on desktop, tablet or phone. It is a turn based strategy board game about outlaw motorcycle clubs fighting for control of Los Angeles, built for players hunting for games like the out of print Sons of Anarchy: Men of Mayhem board game.

How it plays

Two to six clubs, any mix of human players and AI, fight for territory across six rounds. Each round you spend Orders to recruit Members and Recruits, move onto the map's Sites to work them for cash, guns and narcotics, trade on The Silk Road, and manage your Felonies before the law comes down on you.

When two clubs land on the same Site it triggers a Brawl: call for backup, pull guns, then roll, played out as a cinematic 3D fight rather than a card flip. Whoever holds the most Cash at the end of round six wins. Territory is how you earn that cash, not the win condition by itself.

Four ways to play board games online

  • Solo against the AI
  • Online multiplayer across devices, so it works as one of those online board games with friends who are not in the room
  • Local pass and play on one screen
  • Party mode: the 3D board on a TV while everyone plays privately from their own phone

Party mode is the part a physical box cannot do. One shared table, private hands, no install, and nobody hunting eBay for an out of print copy.

Status and pricing

Sin of Angels, the SOA game, is in closed beta, and the beta is free to play. It is not publicly playable yet. Beta testers get in early and their bug reports shape the finished game.

After the beta the game becomes paid. A single purchase is planned to cover browser, Steam and Android together, with cross-compatibility between them.
